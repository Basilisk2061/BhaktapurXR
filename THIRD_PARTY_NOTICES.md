# Third-Party Notices

Audit date: **24 October 2026**. Scope: the current tracked tree, repository provenance records and selected official/provider licensing information. This is a provenance and permission-status inventory, not a new license grant or a declaration that the repository is cleared for public release.

**No root source-code LICENSE has been selected.** Third-party assets and research materials retain their own rights. Any eventual code license must exclude these materials and separately licensed map/database adaptations. An UNKNOWN status means permission has not been established here; it does not assert that the asset is unlawful.

## Permission-status key

- **ATTRIBUTION REQUIRED:** the recorded license permits redistribution, modification and commercial use subject to its conditions. Keep creator, source, license and modification notices; apply share-alike where required.
- **SAFE TO REDISTRIBUTE (CC0):** the recorded public-domain dedication permits copying, modification and commercial use without mandatory attribution. Other applicable rights are not automatically cleared.
- **UNCLEAR / UNKNOWN:** origin, applicable terms or permission for this form of distribution is missing. No permission for redistribution, modification or commercial use is inferred.
- **REQUIRES REVIEW:** identified licensing restrictions or missing provenance need resolution before public source distribution.
