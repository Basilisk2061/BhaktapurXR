Research date: 11 October 2026. Target: the present-day/restored **main royal palace square**, not all protected Bhaktapur squares. Source registry: `src/data/reconstruction/sources.ts`. ## Conflicts and research decisions

- **2006 geometry / current target:** source layout is a reference, not proof that every depicted structure survives or has the same extent. Current inventory and restoration documentation support candidate presence; heights/current superstructures remain null. Current presence is dated evidence, not a 2026 field assertion.
- **Vatsala direction:** NHDP describes south facing; archaeology describes west-descending stairs. Store facing 180° and access 270° separately, with sources/uncertainty. These describe different architectural aspects; current as-built entrance verification remains outstanding.
- **Vatsala plinth:** retain 2.25 and 2.75 m; historical component datums do not justify selecting a current total.
- **Silu:** historic platform plan retained as candidate location; rebuilt presence supported by 2022 evidence. No historic upper temple or uninspected photograph converted to a current model.
- **Krishna/Jagannath/Gopinath:** Ward 3 names Krishna and Jagannath separately, but exact Gopinath correspondence is unproven. Do not equate the plan Krishna polygon with either just to fill the inventory.
- **Kedarnath / generic Shiva icon:** inventory supports Kedarnath, but the icon association is unresolved.
- **Multiple Vatsala/bells:** central Vatsala is not the eastern Vatsala icon. Large bell and barking-dog bell are not interchangeable. Both mapping questions remain open.
- Commons image filenames/category subjects can disagree (a Vatsala-named image was categorised as Chyasilin); no filename-only photo identification adopted. A 2024 Commons photo search result was inaccessible and not treated as inspected.
- **Lost buildings:** Lamupati lost after 1934 and Hari Shankar upper temple lack evidence for inclusion as current masses. Store separate historical exclusions. Taumadhi Nyatapola/Bhairavnath remain outside target.

## Scan availability

Published existence of accurate recording is confirmed. No reusable raw square point cloud, transformation, or licence was obtained. Targeted public-repository searches did not establish a whole-square usable dataset; a guardian-lion model is not a square scan. This is an evidence gap, not a reason to fabricate metric accuracy or block the data review phase. No restricted scan, NC-ND figure, satellite tile, or copyrighted photograph is packaged.

## Accuracy policy

VERIFIED requires direct reliable dimensional/geospatial evidence. SOURCE_DERIVED describes explicit identified plan tracing; it does not verify metre scale or contemporary condition. PHOTO_INFERRED requires multiple inspected photographs supporting the inference: none is asserted here. APPROXIMATE includes constraint-based metre positions, analyst plaza envelope and estimated octagon. PLACEHOLDER is reserved for development context: none is added. UNRESOLVED uses null values, explicit direction state and notes, not convenient defaults. Per-field evidence overrides a broad record class when interpreting dimensions.
