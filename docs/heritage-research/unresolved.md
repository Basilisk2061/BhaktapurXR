## Research exclusions and limitations

- A Kathmandu Gopinath academic article was excluded: it is about a different monument.
- An existing palace research file named as a conservation paper was already identified by the project as unrelated protest poetry. It is not used.
- A later academic Chyasilin caption suggests 1992 completion, but its PDF was behind a browser verification barrier. No barrier was bypassed; it cannot establish the date here. Candidate: https://shneiderman-commons.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2025/04/Building-a-cultural-city-OA-version.pdf . First-hand 1987 proposal / 1990 bolt evidence is used instead.
- NTB booklet direct retrieval failed; indexed excerpts are retained only as corroboration/conflict evidence, never as the sole authority for a published claim.
- Exact monument dimensions, founding dates/patrons where absent, unpublished epigraphy, present opening hours and festival/ringing schedules remain UNKNOWN.
- Photo filenames/descriptions can be wrong. Rejected licensed candidates and reasons are retained in `image-rejections.json`; no rejected photo remains packaged.

DoA sources: [2019 report](https://whc.unesco.org/document/171737), [2022 report](https://whc.unesco.org/document/191662). Local bell distinction: [Vatsala](https://www.bhaktapur.com/discover/vatsala-durga-temple/), [large Taleju Bell](https://www.bhaktapur.com/discover/taleju-bell-big-bell/).
