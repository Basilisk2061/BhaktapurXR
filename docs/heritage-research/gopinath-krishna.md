## Historical content and claim traceability

### Overview

This two-roof temple belongs to the western Durbar Square entrance cluster. The Department of Archaeology identifies Gopi Nath with Dwarika or Krishna Temple; the local Radhakrishna name is also documented.

Claim ID: `gopinath-krishna:overview`. Source IDs: `doa19`, `local-gopi`. Locator: DoA 2019 section 1.13, PDF p32; local Radhakrishna identity account.

### History

The 2015 earthquake damaged the sanctum walls. DoA records shoring, replacement of deteriorated ground-floor Sal timber and use of lime mortar in conservation. Its 2022 report describes repair without wholesale dismantling, with the project completed in fiscal year 2076-77 BS. These reports do not establish an original construction year or patron.

Claim ID: `gopinath-krishna:history`. Source IDs: `doa19`, `doa22`. Locator: DoA 2019 section 1.13, p32; DoA 2022 project 2, p44.

### Architecture

The documented form is a two-roof Nepalese temple with brick masonry and important structural timber. Photographs show a broad lower roof, a smaller upper roof and carved timber entrance work.

Claim ID: `gopinath-krishna:architecture`. Source IDs: `doa19`, `photo:gopinath-krishna`. Locator: DoA section 1.13 and licensed entrance-cluster photograph.

### Significance

The temple is dedicated to Krishna. DoA lists Satyabhama, Krishna and Radha in its sanctum, while a local account supplies a different accompanying deity list. The Krishna dedication is secure; the conflicting subsidiary identifications are not silently merged.

Claim ID: `gopinath-krishna:significance`. Source IDs: `doa19`, `local-gopi`. Locator: DoA 2019 section 1.13; local deity discussion.

## Conflicts

- DoA lists Satyabhama/Krishna/Radha; local account lists Balram/Krishna/Radha. Prefer the official report while retaining the disagreement.
